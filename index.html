About us

Built to own great businesses for the long term

Navigator Holdings is a privately held acquisition and operating company. We seek control of established, profitable businesses with loyal customers and recurring demand, then invest in their people, systems and growth.

Our model is simple: buy well, operate with discipline, and reinvest the cash flow. Each business keeps its identity and local leadership, backed by a holding company that brings capital, financial rigor and proven improvement practices.

Hold, not flip

We buy to own. Where we are the best long-term owner, we intend to hold indefinitely.

Recurring, essential revenue

Services and consumables customers rely on, with high switching costs and strong cash conversion.

Compounding improvement

Small, steady operational gains across safety, quality, delivery and cost that add up year after year.

Investment criteria

What we look for

We focus on established, founder- or family-owned companies where a thoughtful ownership transition matters as much as price.

Financial profile

Revenue
$3M – $30M
EBITDA
$1M – $5M
Track record
Consistent profitability, 5+ years operating history
Location
United States; priority on the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic
Situation
Founder retirement, succession, or partial liquidity
Structure
Majority or full control; seller rollover and transition roles welcome

Business characteristics

  • Essential products or services with recurring or repeat demand
  • Diversified customer base and a strong local reputation
  • Tenured employees and a team willing to grow with new ownership
  • Clear opportunities to improve operations, pricing or reach

Outside our focus

  • Turnarounds and binary-risk situations
  • Heavy customer concentration without mitigation
  • Early-stage or speculative technology

Sectors of interest

Light industrial services Maintenance, testing, inspection and specialty contracting
Fabrication & manufacturing Short-run, make-to-order and quality-critical production
Distribution & logistics Specialty distribution, reverse logistics and route-based field services
B2B & property services Compliance-driven, recurring commercial and residential services

How we operate

An operating approach built on continuous improvement

Inspired by the world's best industrial compounders: decentralized leadership in each business, supported by a shared set of practical tools that turn small improvements into durable results.

People & leadership

Daily huddles, visual management and leader standard work. We invest in training and promote from within.

Strategy deployment

A few vital annual objectives cascaded into clear metrics. Every team knows the target, the gap and the owner.

Problem solving

Root-cause analysis and rapid plan-do-check-act cycles. Small experiments over big-bang projects.

Commercial excellence

Customer insight, pricing discipline and pipeline management to grow profitably.

Operations & supply chain

Capacity planning, supplier development and inventory discipline for shorter lead times and less working capital.

Financial clarity

Clean, timely financials and a simple KPI stack that moves management from lagging to leading indicators.

First 30 days

Stabilize

Governance, monthly close, KPI baseline and proactive communication with customers and staff.

Days 30–60

Standardize

Standard work, planning cadence, pricing guardrails and service level commitments.

Days 60–90+

Improve

Targeted improvement events on lead time, rework and quote-to-cash, plus early growth initiatives.
